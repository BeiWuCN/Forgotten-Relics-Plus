# Forgotten Relics Plus

An unofficial port of Forgotten Relics for **Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge**, featuring powerful magical accessories, tomes and weapons integrated with Thaumaturge.

Solve research notes with complexity **2–8** to unlock crafting recipes. Discover abilities such as teleportation, telekinesis, protection and weather control. **Omega Core** research and crafting require **Re:Avaritia** and `omega_core.enabled` (default: `true`). Set it to `false` in `config/forgotten_relics_plus-common.toml` and restart to disable both.

Gameplay behavior is based on [Forgotten Relics: RE](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/forgotten-relics-re) by **Nyonio (NNYYOONNIIOO) and contributors**, with the author's permission. Art assets are based on the original [Forgotten Relics](https://github.com/jss2a98aj/Forgotten-Relics) by **Integral (Extegral / VictorShadow)**.

Compared with its predecessors, Plus targets **Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge**, integrates Thaumaturge's research and charging systems, adds research-note recipe gates and conditional Omega Core research, and reworks projectile targeting, motion and rendering.

**Requires:** Thaumaturge 1.0.2+, Lithostitched 1.8.0+, Botania and Curios.

# 失落遗物学 Plus

Forgotten Relics 的 **Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge** 非官方移植版，加入强大的魔法饰品、典籍与武器，并接入 Thaumaturge 研究体系。

解开复杂度为 **2～8** 的研究笔记，逐步解锁遗物配方，体验传送、念力、防护与天气控制等能力。**欧米伽之核**研究与配方需要 **Re:Avaritia** 和 `omega_core.enabled` 开关（默认开启）。在 `config/forgotten_relics_plus-common.toml` 中设为 `false` 并重启可关闭二者。

本模组的行为参考 **Nyonio（NNYYOONNIIOO）及贡献者**开发的 [Forgotten Relics: RE](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/forgotten-relics-re)，**已取得作者同意**；美术资产参考 **Integral（Extegral / VictorShadow）**创作的原版 [Forgotten Relics](https://github.com/jss2a98aj/Forgotten-Relics)。

与前作相比，Plus 面向 **Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge**，适配 Thaumaturge 的研究与充能体系，加入研究笔记配方解锁和条件加载的欧米伽之核研究，并重做召唤物的觅敌、运动与渲染。

**必需前置：** Thaumaturge 1.0.2+、Lithostitched 1.8.0+、Botania 和 Curios。
