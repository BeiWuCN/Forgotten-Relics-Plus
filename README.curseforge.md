# Forgotten Relics Plus

An unofficial port of Forgotten Relics for **Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge**, featuring powerful magical accessories, tomes and weapons integrated with Thaumaturge.

Solve research notes with complexity **2–8** to unlock crafting recipes. Discover abilities such as teleportation, telekinesis, protection and weather control. **Omega Core** research is available only with **Re:Avaritia** installed.

**Requires:** Thaumaturge 1.0.0+, Lithostitched 1.8.0+, Botania and Curios.

---

# 失落遗物学 Plus

Forgotten Relics 的 **Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge** 非官方移植版，加入强大的魔法饰品、典籍与武器，并接入 Thaumaturge 研究体系。

解开复杂度为 **2～8** 的研究笔记，逐步解锁遗物配方，体验传送、念力、防护与天气控制等能力。**欧米伽之核**研究仅在安装 **Re:Avaritia** 时出现。

**必需前置：** Thaumaturge 1.0.0+、Lithostitched 1.8.0+、Botania 和 Curios。
